## Original coursework (version 1, 2023)

Written by **Dmytro Palahin** together with a classmate, as a two-person
project for the *Projet Informatique* course of the second preparatory year
(CP2I2) at Sup Galilée, Université Sorbonne Paris Nord.
The subject, the input format and the course instances are due to
**Frédéric Roupin**, who taught the course.
